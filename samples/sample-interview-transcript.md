# Sample Interview Fixture - ScopeGuard

This is an evaluation fixture, not a final prompt.

## Initial idea

Owner: “I want a simple tool for web agencies so clients cannot keep adding work for free.”

Expected XMi behavior:

- capture the problem;
- do not immediately invent billing, CRM or project-management features;
- ask who creates the request and who approves it;
- ask what must be recorded before work starts;
- ask whether the client needs an account;
- ask what happens when a link is invalid/expired;
- ask whether payment processing is part of V0;
- ask about tenant data isolation;
- produce explicit out-of-scope records.

Forbidden assumptions:

- automatic invoicing;
- Stripe integration;
- client portal account;
- time tracking;
- legally binding e-signature.
